**Effective Date:** March 20, 2026 (revised)

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Alchemy Academy ("the Game"), developed and operated by RB.Game ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information in connection with our game available on Roblox, Steam, and our Discord community. We are a solo indie development studio committed to protecting the privacy of all players, especially children.

Alchemy Academy is an educational game designed for players ages 8-16. We do **not** directly collect, store, or process any personal information or personally identifiable information (PII) from our players.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 In-Game Progress Data

The only data we store is non-personal, in-game progress data necessary for gameplay. This data is saved using Roblox's ProfileService and includes:

- Lab level
- Cauldron level
- Essence balance
- Inventory items
- Discovered recipes
- Owned cosmetics
- Tutorial completion status

This data is associated with a Roblox User ID (via ProfileService's AddUserId) solely for the purpose of enabling data portability and deletion requests as required by GDPR. We do not use this identifier to track, profile, or contact players.

### 2.2 Analytics

We use only Roblox's built-in AnalyticsService and, for the Steam version, Steam's built-in analytics. These platform-native tools provide aggregated, non-personally-identifiable gameplay metrics (such as session length and feature usage). We do **not** use any third-party analytics SDKs or services that collect PII.

### 2.3 What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

- Names, email addresses, or physical addresses
- Phone numbers
- Photos, videos, or voice recordings
- Precise geolocation data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Cookies or tracking pixels (beyond platform defaults)
- Any information through custom account systems (we have none)

## 3. Third-Party Platforms

Alchemy Academy operates on third-party platforms, each of which has its own privacy policy and data practices. We encourage all players and parents/guardians to review these policies:

### 3.1 Roblox (Phases 1-2)

- **Roblox Privacy Policy:** https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004630823
- Roblox handles all account creation, age verification, parental consent, and COPPA compliance at the platform level.
- All purchases (game passes, cosmetics) are processed entirely through Roblox's payment system. We do not receive or store any payment information.

### 3.2 Steam (Phase 3)

- **Steam Privacy Policy:** https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/
- Steam handles all account management, age verification, and personal data processing.
- All purchases are processed through Steam's payment system. We do not receive or store any payment information.
- We may use only Steam's built-in analytics; no additional third-party analytics are integrated.

### 3.3 Discord (Community)

- **Discord Privacy Policy:** https://discord.com/privacy
- Our Discord community server requires users to be 13 years of age or older, in accordance with Discord's Terms of Service.
- We do not collect any personal data from Discord users beyond what Discord itself provides (e.g., public usernames visible in the server).

## 4. Children's Privacy

### 4.1 Our Commitment

We take children's privacy seriously. Alchemy Academy is designed for players ages 8-16, and we have built our data practices to minimize data collection:

- We do **not** directly collect any personal information from children (or any player).
- We do **not** require players to create accounts with us.
- We rely entirely on the host platforms (Roblox, Steam) to handle age verification and parental consent.

### 4.2 COPPA Compliance (United States)

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) applies to the online collection of personal information from children under 13. Because we do not directly collect personal information from any user, including children, our practices are consistent with COPPA requirements. Roblox, as the platform operator, handles COPPA-required parental consent and age-gating for users under 13.

### 4.3 GDPR Article 8 — Children's Consent (EEA/UK)

Under GDPR Article 8, information society services offered directly to a child require parental consent for processing personal data of children under 16 (or as low as 13 in some EU member states). Because Alchemy Academy targets ages 8-16:

- We rely on the host platform (Roblox or Steam) to obtain and verify parental consent where required.
- We do not independently process personal data that would trigger Article 8 obligations.
- The in-game progress data we store (Section 2.1) is associated only with platform-assigned identifiers and does not constitute personal data processing requiring separate consent under most interpretations. However, out of an abundance of caution, we treat AddUserId-linked data as potentially personal and apply data minimization principles.

### 4.4 UK Age Appropriate Design Code (AADC / Children's Code)

The UK's Age Appropriate Design Code applies to information society services likely to be accessed by children under 18. Alchemy Academy is designed with the following AADC principles:

- **Best interests of the child:** The game is educational and does not exploit children's developmental vulnerabilities.
- **Data minimization:** We collect only the minimum data required for gameplay (Section 2.1).
- **Default settings:** Privacy-protective settings are on by default — we do not track, profile, or target children.
- **Transparency:** This policy is written in clear, age-appropriate language.
- **No detrimental use:** In-game data is used solely for gameplay; never for profiling, behavioural targeting, or advertising.
- **No nudge techniques:** Purchase prompts do not use dark patterns, pressure tactics, or exploitative design (consistent with CARU guidelines).
- **Connected toys/devices:** Not applicable — Alchemy Academy does not connect to IoT devices.

### 4.5 Parental Rights

Parents and guardians who have questions about their child's data should contact the relevant platform directly (Roblox or Steam) for account-level data. For questions about in-game progress data stored by our game, parents may contact us at the address listed in Section 10.

## 5. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area)

For players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

- **Legal Basis:** The limited in-game progress data we store is processed on the basis of legitimate interest (providing the game service) and, where applicable, contract performance (delivering purchased game features).
- **Data Minimization:** We store only the minimum data necessary for gameplay functionality.
- **Right of Access:** You may request a copy of your in-game progress data by contacting us.
- **Right to Erasure:** You may request deletion of your in-game progress data by contacting us. We use Roblox's AddUserId functionality to ensure we can identify and delete data associated with your account.
- **Right to Portability:** You may request your in-game data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Data Transfers:** In-game progress data is stored on Roblox's infrastructure. Please refer to Roblox's privacy policy for information about international data transfers.

For requests related to your Roblox or Steam account data, please contact those platforms directly.

## 6. CCPA Compliance (California)

For California residents, under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

- **Information Collected:** We store only non-personal, in-game progress data as described in Section 2.1.
- **No Sale of Data:** We do **not** sell, share, or rent any personal information, nor do we engage in cross-context behavioral advertising.
- **Right to Know:** You may request details about the in-game data we store.
- **Right to Delete:** You may request deletion of your in-game progress data.
- **Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 9.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

- **In-game progress data** is retained for as long as the player maintains an active account on the relevant platform and the game remains operational.
- If a player does not log in for an extended period, their data may be retained in accordance with Roblox's or Steam's data retention policies.
- **Deletion requests** for in-game progress data can be submitted to us at any time. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.
- Upon shutdown of the game, all in-game progress data stored via ProfileService will be deleted.

## 8. Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event that in-game progress data is compromised:

- **EEA/UK (GDPR):** We will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach involving personal data. If the breach is likely to result in a high risk to affected individuals, we will also notify those individuals without undue delay.
- **United States:** We will comply with applicable state breach notification laws (including Wyoming's W.S. § 40-12-501 et seq.) and notify affected users as required.
- **All jurisdictions:** We will post a notice in the game's Roblox description and Discord community server.

Because we store only non-personal in-game progress data (Section 2.1), the risk of a breach involving personal information is minimal. The platform operators (Roblox, Steam) are responsible for breaches of account-level personal data under their own privacy policies.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will:

- Update the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
- Post a notice in the game's Roblox description and/or Steam store page.
- Announce the change in our Discord community server.

We encourage players and parents/guardians to review this policy periodically.
