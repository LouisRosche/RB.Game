## Tax Planning

**Set aside 30–35% of ALL income for taxes. Do this immediately.**

As a US sole proprietor / LLC (pass-through taxation):
- Self-employment tax: 15.3% on net profit up to ~$170,000
- Federal income tax: 10–22% bracket depending on total income
- State income tax: varies (Wyoming LLC has 0% state income tax if you live there)

**Quarterly estimated tax due dates** (US):
- Q1: April 15
- Q2: June 16
- Q3: September 15
- Q4: January 15

**Recommended**: Open a separate "tax savings" account. Transfer 30% of every payment received on the same day it arrives.

**Track ALL business expenses** — they're deductible:
- Software subscriptions
- Equipment (computer, monitor — partial if shared personal use)
- Internet (partial)
- Education (books, courses related to game dev)
- Advertising/marketing costs

**Tools**: Wave Accounting (free) or Bench.co (paid bookkeeping). File Schedule C with Form 1040.
