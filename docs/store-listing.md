## Long Description (~500 words for the Creator Hub game page)

```
Alchemy Academy is a free, educational idle simulator where every potion
you brew unlocks a piece of real science.

🌱 GROW magical ingredients with four unique plants:
  • Moonpetal — teaches bioluminescence (why fireflies glow)
  • Fireroot — teaches geothermal energy (Iceland's power source)
  • Shadowmoss — teaches light absorption (Vantablack, the blackest material)
  • Starbloom — teaches nuclear fusion (the sun's power source)

⚗️ BREW — combine two ingredients at your cauldron to discover one of 10 potions.
The discovery is the reward. What does Moonpetal + Starbloom make?

🎲 MUTATE — every harvest rolls a rarity. Common (60%) through Legendary (1%).
We show you the exact odds in the Alchemist's Journal. No hidden mechanics.
No loot boxes. Just fair probability and the thrill of the roll.

📖 DISCOVER — the Alchemist's Journal tracks every recipe you've found and
explains the real science behind each one. 10+ science facts, written for ages 8+.

🏆 COMPETE — the leaderboard tracks Total Gold Earned and Potions Brewed.
Watch your rank rise as you upgrade your lab and cauldron.

🔬 ANTI-DARK-PATTERN DESIGN:
  • No FOMO timers — plants grow while you're offline and wait until you return
  • No pay-to-win — all cosmetics are optional, zero gameplay advantage
  • Transparent odds — every probability is shown in the Journal
  • No guilt notifications — only warm, celebratory messages

5 lab levels × 5 cauldron levels × 10 recipes × 5 rarity tiers =
thousands of unique outcomes. This is systems design, not content grind.

Free to play forever. No purchase required for full gameplay.
```
