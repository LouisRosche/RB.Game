# Trademark & Domain Research — Alchemy Academy

*Phase 0 checklist item: "Search 3 candidate game names on USPTO TESS"*
*Last updated: 2026-03-07*

Note: This document covers the 3 candidate names that were evaluated before
"Alchemy Academy" was selected. Formal trademark registration is a Phase 3
action (file at least 6 months before Steam launch — see business/legal/checklist.md).

## Candidate Names Evaluated

### Candidate 1: Alchemy Academy ✅ SELECTED

**USPTO TESS search guidance:**
- Search term: `ALCHEMY ACADEMY` in the Basic Word Mark Search
- Also search: `ALCHEMY` alone, `ACADEMY` alone to find potential conflicts
- Key classes to check: **Class 41** (Entertainment services) and **Class 9** (Software)
- USPTO TESS: tess.uspto.gov

**Known landscape:**
- "Alchemy" is a common word used in many game titles (Alchemy Stars, Little Alchemy, etc.)
  — a compound mark "Alchemy Academy" is more distinctive than either word alone
- "Academy" is heavily used in educational software but less so in games specifically
- The combination is relatively distinctive in the game space

**Domain status (check before registering — these change):**
- `alchemyacademy.com` — search at whois.domaintools.com
- `alchemyacademy.game` — high likelihood of availability (newer TLD)
- `alchemyacademy.gg` — moderate likelihood of availability
- `alchemyacademy.io` — moderate likelihood of availability

**Risk assessment:** Medium-low. The compound mark is distinctive. Conduct a
full TESS search before any public announcement or Steam page.

### Candidate 2: Potion Lab

**Why it was considered:** Short, immediately descriptive, directly communicates
the core mechanic.

**Why it was not selected:**
- "Potion" + "Lab" combination is saturated in mobile games
- Multiple games exist with similar names (Potion Craft: Alchemist Simulator is
  an established Steam title — direct confusion risk)
- Low trademark registrability as a descriptive name
- Domain availability: `potionlab.com` almost certainly taken

**USPTO concern:** Potion Craft (Niceplay Games) holds a strong market position
in the "potion-making game" space. A name that close invites confusion and
potentially C&D risk even without a registered trademark.

**Verdict:** Eliminated due to conflict risk.

---

### Candidate 3: Brew & Discover

**Why it was considered:** Captures both the core mechanic (brewing) and the
emotional hook (discovery). Action-oriented name.

**Why it was not selected:**
- Feels like a feature description, not a brand
- Difficult to search/find (ampersand in names causes problems)
- "Brew" is associated with beverage brands, not games — causes type confusion
- Less memorable than a two-noun compound

**USPTO concern:** "&" in trademarks creates filing complexity. The name is
also arguably descriptive (describes what you do in the game), which makes
trademark registration harder and provides weaker protection.

**Verdict:** Eliminated due to weak brandability and registrability.

## How to Conduct the TESS Search (do this before Phase 3)

1. Go to **tess.uspto.gov**
2. Select **Basic Word Mark Search (New User)**
3. Search for `ALCHEMY ACADEMY` — look at results in Classes 9 and 41
4. Then search `ALCHEMY` alone in Class 41 — note any live marks
5. Then search `ACADEMY` alone in Class 41 — note any live marks
6. Export results or screenshot for your records

**What to look for:**
- **Live** marks (dead marks don't block registration)
- **Class 41** (Entertainment / Education services — this is where games live)
- **Class 9** (Computer software — also relevant)
- Marks that are "confusingly similar" in sound, appearance, or meaning

**If conflicts found:** Consult an IP attorney before filing. A cease-and-desist
from a prior mark holder is much cheaper to handle before you have brand equity.
